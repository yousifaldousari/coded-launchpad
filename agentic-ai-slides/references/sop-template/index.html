SOP Template

An SOP tells your agent how one job is done, step by step. Every SOP has the same 7 parts.

  1. In Notion, open Finance's row in the AI Team database.
  2. Add a new page inside it, named after the job (for example, "Read a supplier invoice").
  3. Copy the template below into that page, then fill in each part.
  4. Give Finance the link to the page.
